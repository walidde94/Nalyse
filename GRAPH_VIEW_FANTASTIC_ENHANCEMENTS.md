### New Keyframe Animations:

2. **`@keyframes pulse`** - Gentle pulsing for center orb
   - Opacity: 0.5 → 0.8 → 0.5
   - Scale: 1 → 1.1 → 1

3. **`@keyframes glow`** - Pulsing glow for logo

4. **`@keyframes shimmer`** - Text shimmer effect

5. **`@keyframes fadeInRight`** - Control panel entrance
