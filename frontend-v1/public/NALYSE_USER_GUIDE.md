# Nalyse User Guide: Master Your Strategic Intelligence

Welcome to **Nalyse**, the premium command center for enterprise data intelligence. This guide will help you navigate the platform's advanced functions and transform raw data into actionable strategic projects.

---

## 1. The Dashboard: Your Executive Pulse
The Dashboard is your starting point. It provides a real-time "Pulse" of your entire workspace.

![Dashboard Overview](artifact:guide_dashboard_overview)

### **Key Functions:**
- **Enterprise Pulse:** View aggregated metrics across all your datasets. 
    - **Revenue Velocity:** Real-time growth tracking.
    - **Anomalies:** Automatic detection of risks or outliers in your data.
    - **Projected ROI:** AI-calculated value waiting to be realized.
- **Dataset Management:** Upload new CSV/JSON files or organize them into folders (Groups).
- **Storage Tracking:** Keep an eye on your organization's storage limits.

---

## 2. Nexus AI: The Strategic Oracle
Nexus AI is the heart of Nalyse. It doesn't just show data; it **understands** it and suggests business strategies.

![Nexus AI Synthesis](artifact:guide_nexus_ai_synthesis)

### **How to use Nexus AI:**
1. **Select Sources:** Choose which datasets you want the AI to analyze.
2. **Define Goal:** Select a strategic pillar like "Cost Optimization" or "Expansion Strategy".
3. **Synthesize:** Click the **Synthesize Strategic Intelligence** button to start the neural cross-referencing process.
4. **Action:** Review the generated "Strategic Findings" and click **Execute Strategy** to move them to your project board.

---

## 3. Analysis View: Deep Data Exploration
When you need to dig into the details, the Analysis View provides professional-grade tools for every data type.

![Visual Builder](artifact:guide_visual_builder)

### **Primary Tools:**
- **Visual Builder:** A drag-and-drop interface to create custom charts in seconds.
- **Geographic Mapping:** Automatically plot location data onto interactive maps.
- **Graph Connections:** See how different data points (customers, products, regions) relate to each other.
- **Advanced Analytics:** Access specialized tools for **Forecasting**, **Cohort Analysis**, and **Funnel Tracking**.

---

## 4. Strategic Board: From Insight to Action
Once the AI identifies an opportunity, it moves to the Strategic Board. This is where your business execution happens.

![Strategic Board](artifact:guide_strategic_board)

### **Project Stages:**
- **In Execution:** Active projects that are currently being implemented.
- **Value Realized:** Completed projects where the ROI has been successfully captured.
- **Drill-Down:** Each project card contains specific "Key Actions" and "Expected Impact" generated by the AI to guide your team.

---

## 5. Pro Features: Executive Command
To maximize your productivity, use these built-in executive shortcuts:

- **Command Palette (⌘ K):** Press `⌘ K` anywhere to open the search bar. Ask Nexus AI questions like *"Show me revenue by region"* or navigate to any feature instantly.
- **Data Connectors:** Connect live data sources (Google Sheets, SQL, Shopify) for real-time automated updates.
- **Language Mode:** Switch between English and German (or Arabic) seamlessly using the language switcher in the top bar.
